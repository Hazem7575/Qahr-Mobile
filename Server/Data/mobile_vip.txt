# VIP window of the mobile client (Networking\Mobile\MobileVipUi.cs, docs\VIP_UI.md).
# Re-read when saved (no restart). One line = one row of the window (max 11 rows per section, about 70 characters each).
# [level N] = the privileges shown on the "VIP N" tab (levels 1..6). [how] = the "Get VIP" tab.
# <color=0xffRRGGBB>text</color> colors a part of a line. Do not use the character | in a line.

[how]
VIP gives you the city teleport, team teleport, repair all
and the remote warehouse anywhere, from VIP 2 and up.
<color=0xfffced9c>How to get VIP:</color>
- Use a VIP Token or a VIP Box item (VIP 4 / VIP 6, from the
  shop and from events). Each one adds time to your VIP.
- Vote for the server: every vote gives VIP 4 for 2 hours.
When the time ends your VIP goes back to the free level 1.

[level 1]
Free level for every player.
More friends: up to 55 friends.
Offline Training up to 16 hours.
Paid services (teleport, repair, warehouse) need VIP 2.

[level 2]
<color=0xff02f402>City Teleport</color> to the 5 main cities.
<color=0xff02f402>Repair All</color> your equipment from anywhere.
<color=0xff02f402>Remote Warehouse</color>: open any city warehouse.
<color=0xff02f402>Team Teleport</color>: take your team with you.
Send 2 free flowers a day.
More friends: up to 60 friends.
Offline Training up to 17 hours.
+10% Blessing time when a Praying Stone is used.

[level 3]
All the VIP 2 services.
Send 3 free flowers a day.
More friends: up to 70 friends.
Offline Training up to 18 hours.
+15% Blessing time when a Praying Stone is used.
Free Labyrinth next floor every 5 days.

[level 4]
All the VIP 3 services.
VIP hairstyles at the Barber and VIP avatars.
Send 5 free flowers a day.
More friends: up to 80 friends.
Offline Training up to 19 hours.
+20% Blessing time when a Praying Stone is used.
1 free entry to Frozen Grotto 5th floor a day.
Extra daily quests for Study Points.

[level 5]
All the VIP 4 services.
Send 10 free flowers a day.
More friends: up to 90 friends.
Offline Training up to 21 hours.
+30% Blessing time when a Praying Stone is used.
3 free entries to Frozen Grotto 5th floor a day.
Free Labyrinth next floor every 3 days.

[level 6]
All the VIP 5 services, the highest VIP level.
Send 30 free flowers a day.
More friends: up to 100 friends.
Offline Training up to 24 hours.
+30% Blessing time when a Praying Stone is used.
5 free entries to Frozen Grotto 5th floor a day.
Free Labyrinth next floor every 2 days.
